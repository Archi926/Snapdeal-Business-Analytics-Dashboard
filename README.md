###Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end Business Analytics Dashboard developed in Microsoft Power BI using a Snapdeal e-commerce dataset. The dashboard provides actionable business insights by analyzing pricing strategies, customer satisfaction, promotional effectiveness, inventory risk, customer trust, and dynamic pricing segmentation.

The objective was to transform raw e-commerce data into interactive business intelligence dashboards that support data-driven decision-making for pricing, marketing, and inventory management.

###Project Objectives
Analyze pricing strategies across different product categories.
Identify products with high financial risk.
Evaluate the effectiveness of promotional campaigns.
Study the relationship between discounts, ratings, and customer reviews.
Build trust-based product evaluation metrics.
Develop dynamic price segmentation using percentile-based pricing.

###Dashboard Features
###Task 1 – Pricing vs Satisfaction Exception Detection
Identified products with:
Higher-than-average prices
Lower-than-average ratings
Top 20% financial risk
Below-category median sales

Business Value:
Detects products requiring price revision, quality improvement, or discontinuation.

###Task 2 – Promotion Effectiveness Trend Analysis
Calculated baseline discounts.
Measured promotional lift.
Visualized discount trends over time.

Business Value:
Helps evaluate promotional campaign effectiveness.

###Task 3 – Discount vs Rating Causation Analysis
Scatter plot with trend analysis.
Review-weighted customer rating analysis.
Customer satisfaction evaluation.

Business Value:
Demonstrates that discounts alone do not guarantee higher customer satisfaction.

###Task 4 – Financially Risky Inventory KPI
Created Risk Score.
Calculated Inventory Value at Risk.
Built KPI indicators for financial exposure.

Business Value:
Identifies products posing the highest financial risk.

###Task 5 – Trust-Weighted Rating Index
Developed Trust Score.
Calculated:
Simple Average Rating
Review-Weighted Rating
Trust-Weighted Rating

Business Value:
Provides a more reliable measure of product quality by incorporating customer review volume.

###Task 6 – Context-Aware Dynamic Price Banding
Built percentile-based dynamic price segmentation.
Implemented 30th and 70th percentile thresholds.
Demonstrated filter-context-aware pricing analysis.

Business Value:
Enables fair product comparisons across categories and changing market conditions.

###Key Business Insights
High discounts do not necessarily improve customer satisfaction.
Trust-weighted ratings provide a more reliable measure than simple ratings.
Dynamic price segmentation offers better pricing insights than fixed price ranges.
Financial risk KPIs help prioritize inventory management decisions.
Promotional effectiveness should be evaluated using baseline discount comparisons.

###Project Enhancement
The original source code and dataset structure were not sufficient to complete all the required business analytics tasks. Therefore, additional DAX measures, calculated columns, custom KPIs, and data transformations were developed to extend the existing implementation and satisfy all project requirements. These enhancements enabled advanced business analysis, dynamic calculations, and interactive reporting beyond the capabilities of the original source code.
